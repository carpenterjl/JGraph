Getting started with K9

  1. Install the JGraph Runtime 0.2.3, or set JGRAPH_RUNTIME_ROOT to its folder.
  2. In a .NET 8 project, reference the runtime's extern\dotnet\net8.0\JGraph.MATLAB.Runtime.dll and, for the strongly typed interface, K9.dll.
  3. Open the archive with dynamic matlab = MATLABRuntime.StartMATLAB("K9.ctf"); and call its functions as members of matlab, or through the interface's MATLABFunctions and classes.
