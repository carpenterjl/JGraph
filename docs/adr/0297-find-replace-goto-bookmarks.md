# ADR 0297 — The editor's Find and Replace, Go To's line and bookmarks

## Status

Accepted, 2026-11-01. Stage W9a of the App Designer window plan. W9, the editor's tools, is split into W9a (Find and Replace, Go To's line, bookmarks), W9b (Comment, Uncomment, Wrap Comments, Smart Indent, Increase and Decrease Indent) and W9c (code folding, Highlight Current Line, Line Numbers, zoom, the split document, Print).

## Context

The Code View's editor had AvalonEdit's search panel and nothing to replace with; the shell's Edit menu had the same Find. R2025b's App Designer edits in the rich text component, whose code is its own bundle, `toolbox/matlab/rich_text_component/release/bundle.mwBundle.rtc_R2025b.js`, not App Designer's: App Designer's EDITOR tab binds each of its actions to one of the component's by `appDesignerEditorActionId` (`appdesigner/action/EditorAction`; `rtc_find_dialog`, `rtc_goTo_dialog`, `rtc_toggle_bookmark` and the rest).

The component's code was read, and run under node where it stands alone: `findandreplace-ui/FindStrip`, `FindReplaceUtility` and `l10n/nls/findDialog`; `rtc/plugins/find/FindUtilities`, `FindReplaceActions`, `FindHandler`, `FindDialogHandler`, `FindAltShortcutsHandler`, `FindInSelectionHandler`, `FindSeedSearchHandler`, `FindReplaceRegexHandler`, `FindWrapAroundHandler`, `FindPreference`, `FindResult`, `FindConfiguration`, `BinarySearchUtility` and `ReplaceHandler`; `rtc/plugins/goto/GotoHandler`, `GotoUtilities`, `view/GotoDialog` and `nls/gotoDialog`; `rtc/plugins/bookmarks/Bookmarks`, `BookmarksPreferences` and `BookmarksTag`; `rtc/RichTextComponentFeatureEnum` for the actions and `nls/actions` for their words and keys. R2025b's settings were read headless (`ui-probes/w9/w9_settings.m`): `matlab.editor.find.WrapAround` true, `SeedSearchFromSelection` true, `SeedSearchFromCursor` false, `MoveCursorOnType` true, `HighlightAllResults` false, `HighlightSelectionMatches` false, `PersistHistoryAcrossSessions` true, `AddSpaceOnTop` false.

## Decision

- **The strip** (`FindStrip`, `FindHandler`): Ctrl+F opens it over the text's top right corner, seeded with the selection's first non-empty line (`getFirstNonBlankText`) or else the last text searched; Ctrl+H opens it with the replace row, which the session remembers; a selection spanning lines opens it with the count alone. Open already, it is seeded again. Typing finds as you type; Enter finds the next and Shift+Enter the previous; Down opens the history (twenty texts, the most recent first, an empty one kept but not listed); Esc closes it from its boxes or from the text. Alt+M, W, X and S are Match case, Whole word, Regular expression and Find in selection; with the replace row, Alt+R and A are Replace and Replace All. Its result reads `n / m`, `?` when no occurrence is current, `19999+` past R2025b's dead end. The options and both histories are the session's, shared by every editor's strip as `FindPreference` is one.
- **The search** (`FindUtilities`): plain text without case unless Match case; Whole word by R2025b's list of word-breaking characters, a text holding none of them made into `(?:^|\s|[...])text(?=$|\s|[...])` and the character it takes before the word given back; a regular expression's matches in turn, an empty one passed over. From the caret, the first occurrence at or after it, or before it going backward; none ahead and the search goes round. Typing searches from the current occurrence, back by the text's length, so what was found stays found; Find Next and Find Previous step on from the cached result; a caret moved by hand forgets the current occurrence. F3 and Shift+F3 search again with the last text and options whether the strip is open or not; Ctrl+F3 searches for the selection's first line, plainly unless the strip is open with options.
- **Replace** (`FindReplaceActions`): Replace replaces the selection when it is the text (without case) or, for a regular expression, the current occurrence, with `$1`, `$&` and `$$` filled in as JavaScript fills them and a doubled backslash made one; then finds the next. Replace All replaces from the last occurrence back to the first as one undo step, **passing over an occurrence on a line that may not be edited** (`isCursorReadOnly`): App Designer's generated code stays. "Replaced N occurrences." Find in selection holds the search to the selection's lines, and falls back off with no selection.
- **A regular expression refused** (`FindReplaceRegexHandler`): "Invalid regular expression" for one that cannot be compiled; `\n`, `\r` or `\r\n` in it says a multiline search is not supported, in R2025b's words.
- **Go To** (`GotoDialog`, `GotoUtilities`): the dialog under the EDITOR tab's Go To, LINE, and Ctrl+G: "Line number:", the line last typed, OK disabled and "The value entered is not valid." shown unless JavaScript's `Number()` reads a whole number above zero (`3.0`, ` 5 `, `5e0` and `0x5` pass; `3.5`, `abc` and `` do not); the line held to the text's; the caret at its first column, a line out of view brought to the middle.
- **Bookmarks** (`Bookmarks`): Ctrl+F2 sets or clears the caret's line's, an empty line taking none; F2 goes to the next below and round to the first, Shift+F2 to the one above and round to the last; a marked line that became empty is passed over; Next and Previous are enabled only with a bookmark. The margin shows only while the document has one. Each mark is an anchor in the text, so it follows its line and goes with it. The shell keeps a file's marks as R2025b keeps them: `bookmarks.xml` beside `settings.json`, a `file` element per path with a `line` per mark, zero-based, written when a mark is set or cleared and read when the file opens.
- **Both windows**: the shell's Edit menu has Find, Replace, Find Next, Find Previous, Find Selection, Go To Line and the three bookmark items; App Designer's EDITOR tab has Find's split button with its three items, Go To's LINE item and Bookmark's split button live (`AD_EDITOR_FIND_NEXT`, `AD_EDITOR_FIND_PREVIOUS`, `AD_EDITOR_FIND_SELECTION`, `AD_EDITOR_GOTO`, `AD_EDITOR_TOGGLE_BOOKMARK`, `AD_EDITOR_PREV_BOOKMARK`, `AD_EDITOR_NEXT_BOOKMARK`), each forwarded to the Code View's editor, which answers whether it can run.
- **What UI Automation reads**: the editor's `ItemStatus` says the caret's line and column and the bookmarked lines, so a window check sees where a tool put the caret without a picture.

## Consequences

- The rules stand free of a window in `JGraph.Scripting/Editing`: `TextSearch` (the search, the step, the replacement, the label, the seed, the refusal), `FindHistory`, `BookmarkRules` and `BookmarkStore`, `GoToLineRules` (with JavaScript's `Number()`); the editor's `FindReplaceStrip`, `BookmarkMargin` and `GoToLineDialog` are in `JGraph.Controls/Scripting`, and `ScriptEditorControl` carries the commands with their keys, so the shell's menus and the designer's toolstrip bind to one list. AvalonEdit's search panel is gone.
- `TextSearch` shares its name with `System.Windows.Controls.TextSearch`: WPF code aliases it. A UI Automation `Toggle` on a `ToggleButton` raises no `Click`, so the strip's toggles listen to `Checked` and `Unchecked` (W7b's lesson, met again). A `UserControl` has no automation peer, so the editor inside is named (`CodeView.Editor`).
- W9b and W9c build on the same control: the EDITOR tab's CODE section and the VIEW tab's sections are still disabled.

## Measured

- **R2025b's own code under node** (`ui-probes/w9/oracle/find.js`, `EditorToolsOracle.json`): 700 searches with every option over seeded lines, each stepped on forward and backward with wrap-around on and off (51 regular expressions R2025b refuses, refused here too); 124 regular-expression replacements with groups; 200 histories; the seed of twelve selections; 300 bookmark runs of toggles, jumps and moves; 27 Go To inputs. `EditorToolsOracleTests` (6) replays them all to the value.
- **Tests**: `EditorToolsTests` (15 with its theories). With the designer, App Designer, toolstrip, settings and editing suites, 1,119 pass.
- **The window check** (`run-w9adesigner.ps1`, JGraph.Application in Release over an `.mlapp` in its Code View): all 73 checks pass - Find's count and caret, Find Next and Previous from the drop-down and the strip, Match case and Whole word counted as R2025b counts them, a regular expression refused and taken, Replace All passing over the generated line and read back from the file, Esc, Go To's refusals and clamp, a bookmark set, gone to, round, cleared and refused on an empty line. I looked at pictures of the strip with its replace row and of the margin's mark.
- Every older designer window check passes on the final source (see the commit).

## Not measured

- **R2025b's own windows**: the strip's and the dialog's looks were not compared; the rules were read and run.
- **The shell's editor in a window**: it is the same control, checked in the designer's window alone.
- **In a window**: Find Selection, the history drop-down, Find in selection, the Alt keys, Replace of one occurrence, F3 and Shift+F3, Ctrl+F2, F2 and Shift+F2 - the tests cover their rules; the check drives the toolstrip's items and the strip's buttons.
- **Bookmarks kept across sessions** in `bookmarks.xml`: the store is tested; a file reopened in the shell was not watched.

## Divergences

- **The strip's and the dialog's looks are JGraph's**: WPF buttons with glyphs where R2025b has its icons, no resize handle, no message bar sliding in; the option buttons' tooltips carry R2025b's words.
- **The regular expression is .NET's, not JavaScript's**: `\w`, `\d` and `\s` take Unicode letters, digits and spaces where JavaScript's are ASCII; a named group's replacement is written `${name}`, not `$<name>`; a syntax only one engine takes is refused or taken by that engine. The refusal says "Invalid regular expression" alone, where R2025b appends its engine's reason.
- **No dead end**: R2025b stops collecting at 19,999 occurrences and searches on by lines; JGraph collects them all and shows the label's `19999+` only.
- **The history is the process's**, not kept across sessions as R2025b's `PersistHistoryAcrossSessions` keeps it; Highlight All Results and Highlight Selection Matches, both off by default in R2025b, are not offered.
- **Find in selection draws no block** over the selection's lines.
- **An app's bookmarks in the designer are the session's**: R2025b keeps a file-backed document's in `bookmarks.xml`; JGraph keeps the shell's files' there, and writes them when a mark changes, not on Save As.
- **The bookmark margin's mark and width are JGraph's**, and a click in it toggles the line's mark.
